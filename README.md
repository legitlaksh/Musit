# 🎵 musit

A free, beautiful music player for Android. Search millions of songs, build playlists, and enjoy a polished listening experience — no subscription needed.

![Android](https://img.shields.io/badge/Android-3DDC84?style=for-the-badge&logo=android&logoColor=white)
![Kotlin](https://img.shields.io/badge/Kotlin-7F52FF?style=for-the-badge&logo=kotlin&logoColor=white)
![Jetpack Compose](https://img.shields.io/badge/Jetpack_Compose-4285F4?style=for-the-badge&logo=jetpackcompose&logoColor=white)

## ✨ Features

- 🔍 **Search millions of songs** — artists, albums, and tracks
- ▶️ **Smooth playback** with background play and notification controls
- 📥 **Offline downloads** — save songs and listen without internet
- 🔀 **Real crossfade** — songs blend into each other (1–12s, adjustable)
- ❤️ **Liked songs, playlists & recents** — your library, your way
- 🎨 **Dynamic themes** — Material You colors, Light / Dark / System modes
- 📝 **Synced lyrics** — sing along with time-synced lyrics
- ⏱️ **Sleep timer** — fall asleep to music (15/30/45/60 min)
- 🎚️ **Equalizer** — tune the sound to your taste
- ⚡ **Playback speed** — 0.5x to 2x
- 📊 **Listening stats** — track what you actually play
- 🏠 **Home widget** — control music from your home screen
- 🎧 **Smart playlists** — mixes based on time of day

## 🛠️ Built With

- **Kotlin** + **Jetpack Compose** (Material 3)
- **Media3 / ExoPlayer** for audio playback
- **MVVM** + Repository pattern, Coroutines/Flow
- **Room** for local database, **Hilt** for DI
- **Coil** for image loading
